A WARM WELCOME

Food brings us
together.

Himalayan Taste is a fictional Nepalese restaurant concept created for a client-side web development project. Our idea is simple: celebrate the food, warmth and generous spirit associated with Nepalese hospitality.

Our imagined menu takes inspiration from family-style meals, aromatic spices, freshly prepared momo and comforting dishes made for sharing. We want every visitor to feel welcome, whether they are discovering Nepalese cuisine for the first time or returning to familiar favourites.

Based in the heart of our project’s London setting, Himalayan Taste brings a little taste of the mountains to the city.
